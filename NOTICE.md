**What this is.** A specification and research package for an **unbuilt** game. It
contains no game code, no engine, and no third-party game assets. Nothing in it has
been compiled, executed, packaged, or playtested.

**Characters.** The seven bot characters are pseudonymous. They are modelled on real
people the project owner used to play with; the handles published here are invented,
so that no real person is publicly characterized. Their skills and personalities are
design targets for fictional characters.

**Reference to the original game.** "Mobile Forces" identifies the 2002 game used as a
design reference. No affiliation or endorsement is claimed. No original assets,
geometry, audio, artwork, or trademarks are reproduced; the map, vehicles, and rules
described here are original designs, and all numeric values are new design hypotheses
rather than measurements of the original.

**Third-party sources.** Public reviews, vendor documentation, and research papers are
cited by link, with authorship and dates attributed to their original publishers.

**Engineering provenance.** The day-one engineering contract was adapted from a
read-only inspection of a separate private project. That project's name, file layout,
and internal records are deliberately not reproduced here; the requirements in this
package are self-contained.
